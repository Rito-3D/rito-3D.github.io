## Description

A Warudo prop can be almost anything in your Warudo scene outside of your environment and characters.

If you want something you can throw or equip, you can use a prop for that.
If you want a custom screen overlay or particle system? Can also be a prop!
Do you want a stage that can be controlled using Warudo blueprints? I recommend that you use a prop for that.

### Includes
* Custom Modeling, Texturing, and Shading
* Rigging
* Physics
* Animations
* Scripting
* Particles
* VFX
* Audio sources
* Additional lights
* Technical support

Please note that not every prop will include everything in this list.

### Requirements

* You must specify if the prop is to be used for Warudo or Warudo Pro
* You must ask for a quote and understand that requests may be rejected
* You must pay 50% upfront before work may begin

### Details
* Estimated to take between 1-2 weeks
* No work will be performed until first payment
* Delivered as a Warudo Prop mod file
* Unitypackage is available on request, but any dependencies cannot be included and must be purchased yourself
* Props may use premade assets from Unity Asset Store, Booth (where licensing allows for it), and other CC0 licensed assets if it makes sense for the prop to use them
