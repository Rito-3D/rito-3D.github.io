## Description

Not satisfied with the Warudo environments available? Request a custom environment!

### Includes
* Environment design and revisions
* Basic Room
* Background Objects
* Terrain
* Directional and point lights
* Baked lightmaps
* Skybox
* Post-processing volumes
* VFX
    * Volumetric lighting and fog
    * Ambient particles
    * Weather effects
    * Water
    * Planar reflections (mirrors and reflective surfaces)
* Ambient sounds
* Custom HUD
* Scripting
* Animators
* Performance Optimization
* Technical support

Please note that not every environment will include everything in this list.

### Important
* Please understand that complex environments will require a more powerful PC to run.

### Requirements

* You must specify if the Scene is to be used for Warudo or Warudo Pro
* You should specify what computer specs you are targeting
    * Graphics Card
    * Processor
    * RAM
* You should specify what performance target you are targeting
    * FPS
    * Resolution
    * Render Scale
* You must ask for a quote and understand that requests may be rejected
* You must pay 50% upfront before work may begin

### Details
* Estimated to take between 2-3 weeks
* No work will be performed until first payment
* Delivered as a Warudo Environment mod file
* Unitypackage is available on request, but any dependencies cannot be included and must be purchased yourself
* Environments are made using premade assets from Unity Asset Store, Booth (where licensing allows for it), and other CC0 licensed assets.
* Custom modeling and texturing is used sparingly and only for simple models
